Selected Writing Samples

Real script excerpts written in professional Audio/Visual (AV) format with on-screen visual directions and timed voiceover lines.

Long-Form YouTube Script

How Shopping Apps Quietly Keep You Addicted

Format: 10-Minute Video Essay · Audience: Tech & Culture viewers · Goal: High viewer retention past the 30-second mark.

10:14 · 1080p
Hook Excerpt

"In 2012, a casino researcher proved that if a slot machine gives you a reward every single time you pull the handle, you get bored in ten minutes..."

Timecode
Visual Cues (On Screen)
Voiceover / Dialogue
0:00 - 0:08 [Hook]
Fast-paced montage of flashing mobile checkout screens, coupon wheel spins, and discount notifications. Sudden cut to quiet black screen.
Narrator: "In 2012, a casino researcher proved that if a slot machine gives you a reward every single time you pull the handle, you get bored in ten minutes. But if it only rewards you sometimes—completely at random—your brain releases three times more dopamine."
0:08 - 0:16 [Twist]
Crisp screen capture of a popular shopping app spinning a daily prize wheel for discount codes.
Narrator: "That experiment wasn't designed for Las Vegas. That’s the exact psychological mechanism running inside the shopping app currently sitting on your phone."
0:16 - 0:25 [Roadmap]
Minimal graphic title card: 'The 4 Traps of Modern E-Commerce'. Slow zoom in.
Narrator: "Today, we're breaking down the four subtle design traps brands use to get you to spend money before you even realize you've opened the app."
Why This Works
Rather than starting with a cliché greeting ("Welcome back to the channel"), it immediately opens with an intriguing psychology experiment, bridges it directly to the viewer's everyday life, and clearly states what the viewer will learn in the next 10 minutes.
Short-Form Video (TikTok / Reels)

Skincare Myth Buster: The "Cold Spoon" Routine

Format: 30-Second UGC Video · Product: Caffeine & Peptide Eye Serum · Angle: Debunking a common home remedy.

0:30 · 9:16
Pattern Interrupt

"Please stop freezing spoons and piling on three layers of concealer every morning. It’s not fixing your puffy under-eyes—it’s just chilling your skin..."

Timecode
Visual Cues (On Screen)
Voiceover / Dialogue
0:00 - 0:04 [Pattern Interrupt]
Creator sits in morning bathroom lighting, holding a frozen metal tablespoon over one eye looking visibly tired. Quick cut to camera.
Creator: "Please stop freezing spoons and piling on three layers of concealer every morning. It’s not fixing your puffy under-eyes—it’s just chilling your skin for five minutes."
0:04 - 0:14 [The Solution]
Cut to creator picking up a sleek dropper bottle, applying two drops under the eye with gentle fingertip tapping.
Creator: "Here's what actually made a noticeable difference for me in seven days: using concentrated caffeine peptides that actually absorb instead of just sitting on top."
0:14 - 0:30 [Call to Action]
Close-up side-by-side comparison photos on screen. Creator smiles naturally and points to the link button below.
Creator: "It takes about ten seconds every morning and it's backed by a 30-day trial. Grab a bottle from the link below before their spring batch sells out."
Why This Works
Starts by calling out a widely recognizable DIY habit (frozen spoons) which makes the viewer feel understood. It presents the product not as a miracle cure, but as a practical, time-saving alternative with zero risk.
Paid Social Ad (Instagram & Facebook Feed)

Clean Energy Drink vs. The 2 PM Slump

Format: Social Feed Copy · Product: Nootropic Focus Drink · Target: Knowledge workers & founders.

Meta Feed Ad
Direct Hook

"Most energy drinks don't give you focus. They give you 200mg of harsh caffeine, a 45-minute spike of restless jitters, and a heavy 2:00 PM crash..."

Section
Creative / Format
Ad Copy & Body
Primary Text
Clean photo of product can on a minimalist desk next to a mechanical keyboard and notebook.
Most energy drinks don't give you focus.

They give you 200mg of harsh caffeine, a 45-minute spike of restless jitters, and a heavy 2:00 PM crash right when you need to get real work done.

QuickFocus was designed differently:
• L-Theanine + Green Tea Caffeine: Clean, calm focus without the jitters.
• Lion's Mane Mushroom Extract: Helps clear brain fog during deep work.
• Zero Added Sugar: No afternoon crash.

One can replaces your second and third cup of coffee—at half the price of a café latte.
Headline & CTA
Feed Card Footer Banner
Headline: Stop crashing at 2 PM. Work smarter with clean energy.
Button: Shop Now (Try Risk-Free for 30 Days)
Why This Works
Instead of reciting ingredients, it attacks the real problem everyone relates to: the 2 PM energy slump and caffeine jitters. It anchors the price against a $6 café latte, making the purchase feel like a money-saving decision.
